# Bounded critique round

Read for consequential or disputed decisions and explicit deep review.

1. Freeze the initial reports and the subject revision. Keep a private mapping
   of authors to report IDs. Anonymize author/model names and self-identifying
   prose where feasible; label candidates A/B/C and vary presentation order.
   Disclose when anonymity is incomplete rather than promising perfect blinding.
2. Supply the same brief, reports and a bounded critique assignment. Ask:
   - Which consequential claims lack support or contradict primary evidence?
   - What failure mode or alternative did every initial report miss?
   - Does the draft preserve the strongest opposing evidence?
   - What observable check would resolve each material disagreement?
3. Judge claim validity against the common criteria. Whole-report ranking is
   optional only when candidates are comparable solutions to the same problem;
   different specialist lenses are not interchangeable contestants.
4. Collect changed positions and reasons. Label all responses that saw peer work
   peer-informed. Do not treat a second round as new independent support.
5. Use a critic to attack the proposed synthesis on a high-stakes decision.
   Prefer a fresh context; if that is unavailable, disclose reused context.
   Do not suggest that a different process alone implies a different provider.
6. Resolve factual disputes with an outside check where available. Preserve
   unresolved value choices and minority evidence in the final recommendation.

Normal bound: one initial round plus one critique round. Choose actual time,
participant and token limits before launch; never add a compulsory waiting period.
An unavailable participant, timeout or failed tool call is recorded as absent.
Reuse existing authorized members for focused clarification; do not recursively
spawn replacements or repeat uncertain external side effects.
